# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - not released yet

First set of models and instructions.

- Six models from one real Technic collection: buggy, dump truck 6x6, racer, robot arm, walker, pendulum clock.
- Laws for every model: every part holds (one connected group), every pin sits at least 15 LDU in two parts, no two
  pins in one hole, bulky parts and beams do not overlap, no part is missing from the collection, every motor has a
  battery box and an IR receiver; model laws (steering, bed tipping, walking feet, gear train).
- PDF instructions for each model in two sizes, iPad mini (1:1 screen) and A4: a cover with the model name, parts,
  pages and build time; new parts with red arrows; sub-assemblies; wiring; all parts; 1:1 axles and beams.
